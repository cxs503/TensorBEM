### 2.1 `src/tensorbem/benchmarks/declared_numbers.py` (3 hunks)

**(a) benchmark-module import aliases — hunk @ L49.**
`main` aliases `floating_hemisphere as hemisphere_benchmark` and
`submerged_sphere_wave as sphere_benchmark`; `agent/m3181` aliases the same two
modules `as hulme_benchmark` / `as wang_linton_benchmark`. Both names are used
by their own `_run_group` arms, so **both alias sets were kept** (5 import lines,
sorted, de-duplicated). Resolver: `import_union`.

**(b) registry tuple tail — hunk @ L536 (the big one, 595 lines on the `main` side).**
`main` appended its new `DeclaredNumber(…)` literals *inside* the `DECLARED_NUMBERS`
tuple; `agent/m3181` had instead closed the literal and appended a generated block
with `) + _free_surface_3d_rows()`. Taking either side alone deletes the other's
registry rows. Resolution keeps **`main`'s 595 inline lines and `agent/m3181`'s
`+ _free_surface_3d_rows()` tail**: the literal is closed after main's last row and
the m3181 generator is concatenated. Resolver: `registry_tuple_tail`.
(`_free_surface_3d_rows` is defined at L287 of the merged file — it came in with
the m3181 side of the merge, so the call resolves.)

**(c) `_run_group` dispatch arms — hunk @ L1237.**
The two sides add *disjoint* benchmark-group arms: `main` → `hulme_hemisphere`,
`submerged_sphere`, `hulme_chief`; `agent/m3181` → `hulme3d`, `hulme3d_chief`,
`wang_linton3d`. No group name collides, so both blocks are kept verbatim
(6 dispatch arms). Resolver: `measure_groups`.

## 5. Notes, limits, traps hit

* the local clone being shallow silently disables three-way merging — `git merge-base`
  just fails; `--deepen` first.
* the editable-install shadowing above is the single most likely cause of a
  false "all green" on this box.
* "mergeable" was a text-level verdict only; the real risk here was semantic
  (registry row loss), which the key-set audit in §2.4 is designed to catch.
* the merged `declared_numbers.py` now imports two modules under two aliases each.
  That is deliberate (keeps both sides' call sites untouched and reviewable);
  a follow-up cosmetic pass could collapse them once reviewers are done.
* the "re-run CI" commits (both sides) carry no tree change; they only perturb
  the merge base.
