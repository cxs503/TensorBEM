## Interpretation

`f_cont` is the Richardson continuum limit of the BEM ladder, i.e. the solver answer with its own discretisation error removed. `E(cont)` is therefore the *reference* error, not the solver error: it measures the multipole series against an independent continuum solution rather than against itself.

`E(1280)` compared with `GCI(1280)` is the ASME V&V 20 validation test: when E <= U_V the discrete result is consistent with the reference *within its own numerical uncertainty band*, which is the strongest statement a single discretisation level can support.
