## The criterion this anchor supports (GAP-ICE-GATE-POWER / chain S07')

The hydrodynamic leg of the chain is judged by *this* rewrite, which carries the
three items the anchor demands:

> **REAL criterion (2-D heave added mass of the ice-going section).**
> (a) *Baseline source*: the high-frequency limit `A_inf` (`phi = 0` on `z = 0`)
> of the surface-piercing section, computed on the mirror-doubled body with the
> load integral over the physical panels only, and pinned against three
> closed-form results -- full circle `rho*pi*a^2` (5.0e-6), full ellipse
> `rho*pi*a^2` (3.8e-4) and half-immersed circle `0.5*rho*pi*a^2` (5.0e-6).
> (b) *Threshold bound to the observed order*: the fine ladder's observed order
> is `p = 1.25` and the finest-pair change is quoted with every value, so the
> 3 % band is compared against a measured discretisation error, not asserted;
> equivalently, at the criterion mesh (32 panels/side) the gate's *minimum
> detectable section defect* is 1.3 % (3 % gate) and 7 % (15 % gate).
> (c) *Rejected negative controls*: un-halved doubling (+100 %), the rigid-wall
> zero-frequency branch used as an `A_inf` baseline (-163.9 %), waterplane-lid
> closure (+83.1 %) and mode-normal mixing (-100 %) are all rejected at 3 %.
> The coarsest ladder level (8 panels/side, +2.6 %) is **not** rejected at 3 %,
> which is why the criterion must pin the mesh and not rely on the band alone.

Consequence for the disputed numbers: the computed `A_inf = 225.35 t/m`
(Richardson, 4 levels) agrees with audit #19234's independent 228.6 t/m to
**+1.44 %**, so the 165 % deviation of the declared `A_inf/L = 86.1 t/m` is
confirmed by a third, independent route; the "2-D/3-D caliber correction to
5.97 %" of report #19477 is **not** reproduced when the declared per-length
coefficient is compared with a converged 2-D `A_inf`.

## Limitations (honest)

1. The `omega -> 0` branch is reported but is **not an admissible exterior
   problem in 2-D**: its mirror data carry a net flux `2 V B = 40 m`, so the
   potential grows logarithmically and the quoted finite value is a
   finite-part artifact. It must never be used as a baseline.
2. The local artifact was produced with the reduced ladder `(8,16,32,64)`, an
   informativeness reference of 64 panels/side and 6 Monte-Carlo draws; the
   128-panels/side ladder point and a larger detection sample are **not_run**
   in this artifact (see the config block in `results.json`).
3. The Monte-Carlo detection probability (0.17 at the 3 % gate) rests on 6
   draws and is a weak statistic; the deterministic defect/deviation curve is
   the primary informativeness measure.
4. No frequency-domain sweep is used here: the surface-piercing section is
   outside the supported domain of `tensorbem.free_surface_solver`, whose
   Green function requires a strictly submerged body.
