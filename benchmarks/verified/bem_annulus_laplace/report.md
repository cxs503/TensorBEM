### 6.1 Inner loop normals not flipped

| case | panels | correct rel L2 q | unflipped rel L2 q | ratio | correct flux err | unflipped flux err |

| radial_log | 64/32 | 1.840e-03 | 1.660e+00 | 902.0x | 1.140e-03 | 1.175e+00 |

Residual of the unflipped system: 3.924e-16 (correct: 3.350e-16).  The linear solve still looks converged, so a residual-only acceptance test cannot catch a wrong loop orientation -- which is why `MultiLoopMesh` enforces it.

### 6.2 Probe inside the hole

Probe `[0.1, 0.0]` refused: **True**

Message: `points must lie in the fluid domain: 1 target(s) fall inside a hole loop, where the Green representation evaluates the analytic continuation and not the field of the problem`

The guard is part of this mission: before it, `evaluate_multiloop_field` returned the analytic continuation of the Green representation for a target inside the hole -- a plausible number for a point that is not in the fluid. `:func:`tensorbem.multicomponent.evaluate_fluid_field`` already refused such points; the multiloop path did not.

### 6.3 Validity limits found by the sweeps

- wall ratios at 64/32 panels missing the 3 % tolerance: none.
- conditioning is worst at `L = 1.00` (5.945e+02 at `a/L = 1.000`): the annulus inherits the logarithmic-capacity cliff of the free-space kernel, it does not remove it.  The default `L = 2` sits at `a/L = 0.50`, i.e. on the safe side but not far from it.
