## 2. Method

Five four-level ladders, constant refinement ratio `r = 2.0`:

| axis | parameter | levels | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| spanwise | `n_radial` | 4, 8, 16, 32 | panel density across the span |
| azimuthal | `wake_steps` | 12, 24, 48, 96 | azimuthal segments of the helical wake; the blade surface carries no circumferential segmentation in a blade-fitted lattice, so this is the model's only azimuthal discretisation knob |
| chordwise | `n_chord` | 1, 2, 4, 8 | panel density along the chord |
| wake_length | `wake_length` | 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 | trailing-vortex extent in revolutions |
| kernel | `core_ratio` | 0.4, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05 | van Garrel vortex-core desingularisation radius; the direct-dense lattice solve exposes no iterative relaxation factor, so this regularisation is the axis that plays that role (declared substitution, see the artefact's limitations) |

Roache GCI with `Fs = 1.25`, evaluated on the three coarsest and the three finest levels of every cell; the reported `p`/`GCI` are the finest triplet. The split is `d_total = d_disc + d_model` with both terms referenced to the same anchor, so it closes by construction (checked to 1e-9 pp).

## 5. Limitations and traps

- No blade-surface circumferential segmentation exists in a blade-fitted lifting-surface lattice, so the mission's "circumferential segments" axis is realised as the azimuthal segmentation of the helical wake (`wake_steps`), the only azimuthal discretisation the model has. Recorded as a declared substitution, not silently renamed.
- The solver is direct-dense with no fixed-point relaxation coefficient; the mission's "relaxation factor" axis is therefore realised as the van Garrel vortex-core desingularisation ratio, the only regularisation parameter. Its fine end (0.05) sits on the code's floor `core = max(core_ratio * segment, 0.5 * span)`, so the axis saturates there and the reported `p` describes the approach to that floor, not to an unregularised kernel.
- The reported GCI is a discretisation band around the *lattice* solution, not an uncertainty on the anchor: the transcribed Table 2 values themselves carry measurement/reading uncertainty that this artefact does not quantify (see the provenance depth field of the anchor block).
- The observed order of the spanwise ladder is negative at every J once the triplet is taken on the fine side (p = -1.520, -1.595, -1.565), while the coarse-side triplet of the same four-level ladder reports p = 2.878, 3.016, 3.050. The lattice is therefore not in the asymptotic range and no GCI from it is a bound; the numbers are reported as measured instead of being fitted to a p >= 1 expectation.
- Only J = 0.5 / 0.8 / 1.0 are ladders; the remaining anchor points keep the single-density evidence of the sweep study (docs/m7485_kp505_openwater_bem.md).
