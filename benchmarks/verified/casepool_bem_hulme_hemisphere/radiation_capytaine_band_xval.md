## 6. Limitations

- capytaine is a low-order constant-panel method as well (Hess-Smith vs TensorBEM's own constant panels), so this axis separates *reference vs solver* but not *low-order panel error* in general;
- no lid was added on the waterplane in this capytaine arm (capytaine 3.x exposes no `add_lid`); the `first_irregular_ka` field of each cell records whether a cell sits near an irregular frequency of the referee, and `G2` guards against blaming a referee that has not self-converged;
- `G5` is reported as `NOT_RUN` when the frozen TensorBEM cells do not cover an in-band cell: `band_extension/` starts at ka = 2.2, so the in-band cross-code check lives in `capytaine_excitation.json` / `independent_axis.py` (all six quantities, ka <= 2) and is not re-run here;
- the referee mesh ladder stops at 4096 panels: above ka = 3 its own drift (6.8-8.6 %) is larger than the 5 % gate, so the correct reading of those cells is an *upper bound* on the referee error, not a certificate;
- the printed tables of Hulme (1982) are paywalled, so no printed digit enters this file: the series is the only reference and its truncation ladder is the evidence that it is converged.
